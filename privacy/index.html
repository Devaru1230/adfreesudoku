Privacy Policy — Ad Free Sudoku

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what Ad Free Sudoku ("the app") does with your information. It is published by Undercurrent Labs ("we", "us"), based in Bengaluru, India.

In short: the app shows no advertisements of any kind, in either the free or the Pro version. It works entirely on your device. We operate no server, there is no account system, and your name, scores and game progress never leave your phone. The only information that goes anywhere is handled by Google if you choose to buy the Pro unlock.

1. Information the app stores on your device

The app saves the following in your device's local browser storage. This information stays on your device, is not transmitted to us, and we cannot see it:

  • Player name — the pseudonym you choose on first launch (for example "Blue Tokai"). This is a nickname, not your real name, and you are free to invent anything.
  • Game statistics — scores, solve times, mistakes, win streaks and your rating.
  • Saved game — your in-progress puzzle, so you can close the app and return to it.
  • Settings — chosen theme, lightning mode preference, hint balance.
  • A randomly generated device identifier — created on your device to distinguish your own records. It is not linked to you, your phone number, or any Google account.
  • Purchase status — whether the Pro unlock has been activated.

Uninstalling the app removes all of it. You can also clear it at any time from within the app: tap the version number in the top bar, then "Reset scores & start clean".

2. Information we collect

None. We operate no servers, run no analytics, and have no way to identify you or contact you unless you choose to email us.

3. Advertising

There is none. The app contains no advertisements, no advertising SDK, and no ad network code — in the free version or the Pro version. Nothing in the app reads your advertising identifier or builds a profile of you.

4. Purchases

The Pro unlock is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or handle your card details, bank information, or billing address. Google provides us only with anonymised sales reports. Refunds are handled under Google Play's refund policy.

5. Sharing your results

The app includes buttons that let you share your score or rank. These open WhatsApp (or your device's share menu) with a message pre-written for you. Nothing is sent until you choose a recipient and send it yourself, and the message contains only your chosen player name and your game statistics.

7. Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, and show no advertising, there is nothing collected from children for us or anyone else to delete.

8. Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or erase. For data collected by Google through Google Play Billing when you make a purchase, please exercise your rights through Google at myaccount.google.com.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's update notes.
